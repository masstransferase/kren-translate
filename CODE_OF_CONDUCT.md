# Code of Conduct

KREN is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and privacy-conscious community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Protect privacy. Never post another person's confidential text, credentials, identity, or private logs.
- Respect provider terms, software licenses, trademarks, and intellectual property.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, credential exposure, spam, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **masstransferase.github@gmail.com**. The repository owner may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, reject pull requests, or restrict participation when needed to protect people and the project. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially on a best-effort basis.

This policy applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, release discussions, and other spaces where someone represents KREN.
